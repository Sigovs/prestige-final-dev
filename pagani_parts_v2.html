Pagani Parts

Pagani parts.
Ordered, not stocked.

Pagani builds in the dozens, not the thousands, and the parts follow the same logic: almost nothing is a shelf item anywhere in the world. What matters is having the relationship with Modena and the chassis number in front of you.

Order a Part +1 (833) 290-5149
Authorized Source

Nothing about this is a catalogue transaction.

On a car built in these numbers the part is identified from the chassis, not from a model year, and much of what you might need is made rather than picked. That is normal for the marque — what is not normal is being told it is unavailable by somebody who has not asked.

Tell us the chassis number and what has happened. We will come back with what the factory holds, what has to be made, and an honest answer about time.
