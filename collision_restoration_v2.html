Certified

Why certification isn't a formality here.

Any body shop can straighten steel. These cars aren't steel — aluminum structures, carbon panels, bonded joints, and factory paint systems that a general shop has neither the training nor the equipment to put back correctly.

Certification means the manufacturer trained our technicians and approved the equipment they work with. Repairs are carried out to manufacturer specification using genuine parts — which is also what protects the car's value. A documented, certified repair is the difference between a car with history and a car with a problem.

Paint

Color, matched — not approximated.

On a factory color that has spent years in the Florida sun, the match is the entire job. A panel that is merely close is a panel that everybody notices, from three feet away, in daylight.

So the booth, the materials, and the time spent mixing are not where we save money — they are the reason the repair disappears.
