Lamborghini Parts

Lamborghini parts,
from the department
that fits them.

South Florida’s authorized Lamborghini dealer, and the largest Lamborghini service department in the country — so the counter here is the factory counter, with factory part numbers and the technicians who install them in the same building.

Order a Part +1 (833) 290-5149
Authorized Source

Why it matters more on this marque than most.

A Lamborghini is carbon, aluminium and a great deal of electronics, assembled to tolerances that a pattern part does not respect. The factory number exists because the factory measured it.

We order against your VIN rather than a model year, which is how you avoid the part that fits the car it was photographed on and not yours. If it is on the shelf, it goes home with you today; if it is not, it is ordered the same afternoon.
